The model behind the verdicts

API: checking...

PhishLens serves a Text-CNN: a small neural network that reads the words of an email in order and learns short phrases that mark phishing. The details below come from the running API, so a screenshot of a verdict can be traced to the exact run that produced the model.

What it can and cannot tell you

The model was trained on six public email collections pooled together. It reads the subject and body only: headers, sender domains and link counts are left out on purpose, because they let a model recognise which collection an email came from instead of whether it is phishing.
